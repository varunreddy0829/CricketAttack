How the Auction Works

60 players are pooled into 12 sets — 3 tiers (Marquee opens at ₹2 Cr, Mid-Level ₹1 Cr, Group ₹0.2 Cr) across 4 roles. With a ₹100 Cr purse, outbid your rival (every bid raises the price by at least ₹0.5 Cr) or fold with "I'm Out". Build a balanced 15–21 squad (at least 1 keeper), then Lock In.

Miss 15 players when the auction ends and you get a 60-second grace period to Auto-Fill — fail to lock a valid squad in time and you auto-forfeit the match.

Once squads are set, each side picks their best 11 (max 4 overseas, at least 1 keeper) before the match begins.
