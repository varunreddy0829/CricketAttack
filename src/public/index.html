CRICKET ATTACK
Two devices, one match — here's the game in 30 seconds
🔨

1 · The Auction

60 stars are pooled into 12 sets — 3 tiers (Marquee 🌟 opens at ₹2 Cr, Mid-Level ₹1 Cr, Group ₹0.2 Cr) across 4 roles. With a ₹100 Cr purse, outbid your rival (every bid raises the price by ≥₹0.5 Cr) or fold with “I'm Out”. Grab a balanced 15–21 squad (needs ≥1 keeper), then Lock In. ⚠️ Miss 15 players when the auction ends and you get a 60-second grace period to Auto-Fill — fail to lock a valid squad in time and you auto-forfeit the match.

👕

2 · Pick Your XI

Choose your best 11 from your squad and set your openers. Max 4 overseas ✈, at least 1 keeper 🧤. This is where the overseas cap bites!

🎯

3 · Play the Match

20 overs a side. Batters set a secret intent — Defensive ↔ Aggressive. The bowling side secretly picks who bowls and their own intent — Defensive ↔ Wicket-Hunting. Both sides are gambling blind — go big for runs or wickets, but the risk cuts both ways!

🏆

4 · Win It

Bat first to post a target, or chase it down. Most runs wins. Lose all 10 wickets or run out of overs and your innings is done. Simple as that.
